**Result.** The autoscaler saw 80% CPU against a 60% target within 30 seconds and went from 2 web pods to the maximum of 6 within about a minute. Autopilot added nodes for the new pods by itself. Throughput rose from about 600 to about 1,350 requests a second. Of 354,036 requests, 137 failed (0.04%), all between t=30 s and t=120 s, when the two original pods were saturated and the new ones were still starting. I did not record what kind of failure they were. After that there were no failures for the remaining three minutes.

CPU stayed near 95% even at 6 pods, so this load is more than the configured maximum can absorb comfortably. With a real workload like this I would raise `maxReplicas`.

**A mistake in my first run.** My first load generator opened a new connection for every request. After about 40 seconds the PC had no free ports left, and 194,802 of 246,235 requests failed on my side without ever reaching the cluster. The autoscaler still scaled to 6 pods in that run, but CPU then fell to 3% because almost nothing was arriving. I changed the generator to keep each connection for 50 requests (short enough that new pods still receive traffic) and ran the experiment again. The numbers above are from the second run; the first run's output is kept in `evidence/first-run-*.txt`.
