**Result.** All 373 requests returned 200 while one web pod and then one links pod were deleted. The slowest response was 248 ms. Kubernetes started a replacement for each pod, and both replacements were running by the time the snapshot above was taken (the two pods with ages of 63 s and 38 s). Deleting a pod this way is a graceful shutdown: the pod is taken out of the Service first and the `preStop` wait gives requests in flight time to finish. A node that dies without warning would be harsher than this test.
